What do these settings mean?

Preset fills in the paper type and size for common rolls and labels. Changing a size afterwards switches it to "Custom".

Paper width is the physical width of your roll or label backing. It is only used to draw the preview to scale.

Print width is the width of the area that gets printed: the label width, or up to 48 mm on a roll. It can't go above 48 mm, because the print head is 48 mm wide (384 dots at 8 dots per mm). On a 52 or 58 mm roll, the rest is a blank margin.

Feed after print (roll only) is how much paper comes out after the last printed line, so you can tear it off. The printer always feeds a little on its own so the print clears the tear-off edge; this field adds extra paper on top of that.

Label height is the length of one label. On a continuous roll the length follows the content instead.

Darkness 13 gives the blackest result. Higher values make this printer print faster and lighter. Large solid black areas always come out a little grey.
